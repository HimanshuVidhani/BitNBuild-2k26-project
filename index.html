The Problem

Compliance data lives in five different places — so it's rarely checked at all.

Fragmented verification, fake or expired documents, manual bottlenecks at checkpoints, and aging vehicles pushing emissions higher all stem from one root issue: no single, trusted source of truth.

The Solution

A single digital ecosystem, built on the databases that already exist.

EcoScan combines IoT, OCR, AI-driven number plate recognition and direct links to VAHAN/Parivahan to deliver compliance auditing in real time — for citizens, RTOs and enforcement alike.

What the platform does

Six connected modules cover everything from roadside scanning to state-level enforcement analytics.

ANPR & OCR Scanner

A phone camera or CCTV feed extracts a plate number and pulls live compliance data in milliseconds.

The 4-Check Dashboard

PUC, insurance, fitness/RC and road tax or challans — one health card per vehicle, always current.

Dynamic Eco-Score

Age, fuel type and maintenance history combine into a score, shown as a green, yellow or red badge.

Automated Alerts

SMS and WhatsApp reminders reach owners weeks before a certificate expires — and fleet operators the moment a vehicle falls out of line.

Enforcement Portal (B2G)

Traffic police and RTOs run mass checkpoint checks, issue digital challans, and see pollution heatmaps by district.

Secure API Gateway

Insurers, marketplaces and lenders verify a vehicle's legal standing instantly before underwriting or onboarding.

Cleaner air

Timely maintenance is enforced and high-emission vehicles are identified and taken off the road faster.

Economic efficiency

Paperless verification cuts checkpoint delays and closes gaps that used to enable corruption and revenue loss.

Safer roads

Only insured, fit and legally authorized vehicles stay in circulation on Indian highways.

About EcoScan India

Cleaner Roads | Greener Tomorrow Initiative

EcoScan India is a next-generation smart transportation compliance and vehicular emissions monitoring platform. Designed to integrate directly with national registries like VAHAN, Parivahan, and DigiLocker, EcoScan empowers millions of vehicle owners and traffic enforcement departments to transition towards zero-emission non-polluting roadways.

Key pillars include instant optical plate verification (ALPR), cryptographically verified green QR tags, proactive PUC renewal notifications, and emission compliance mapping.
